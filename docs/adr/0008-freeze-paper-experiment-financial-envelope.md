# Freeze a measured financial envelope before formal paper runs

The GPT-4.1 Mini paper experiments use hard allocations of USD 400 for the four-benchmark primary matrix, USD 110 for the CLUTRR mechanism ablation, and USD 70 for explicit execution-failure recovery and unexpected metered overhead.  Disjoint pilots meter the official method paths and freeze concurrency before formal launch; the ceilings are not spending targets, unused funds do not authorize extra seeds, and financial controls never interrupt an in-flight rollout or alter optimizer semantics.
